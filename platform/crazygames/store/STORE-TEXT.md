## Title

Talon Strike

## Short description (one line)

Fly a helicopter gunship through nine missions and endless contracts. Hunt SAM sites, convoys, gunboats and a stolen prototype.

## Description

Skywatch needs a pilot. Take the controls of an attack helicopter and fight a militia across six war zones: desert, valleys, tundra, jungle, volcanic oil fields and red canyons. Every theatre has its own island, weather and enemies painted in its own camouflage.

Each mission asks for something different. Knock out SAM sites, stop an arms convoy at night, blow up fuel depots, sink gunboats, drop a shield and flatten the bunker behind it, and hunt down RED TALON, a stolen prototype gunship. Then rescue downed aircrew, escort aid trucks and hold your base against five waves.

When the campaign is done, the war isn't. Contracts are endless missions built fresh every time, with a rank that climbs as you clear them. Higher ranks bring tougher enemies, more targets, bigger pay and twists like night ops, no flares or a hard time limit. A new daily contract arrives every day, the same for every pilot, and clearing it on the days in a row builds a streak.

**Features**

- A nine-mission campaign across six theatres, then a second, tougher tour
- Endless Contracts with ranks, high-risk offers and a daily contract with a streak
- Four aircraft: the all-round Stormwolf, the fast Kestrel scout, the heavy twin-rotor Titan and the Viper jet-copter, unlocked by finishing the campaign
- Chain guns, homing missiles, cluster bombs, flares to break missile locks, and turbo
- A workshop of upgrades for armour, guns, missiles, flares and engines
- Up to three stars a mission for clearing it fast and staying in one piece
- Night missions, sandstorms, snow, rain and falling ash
- An original synth soundtrack for every theatre
- Keyboard and mouse, twin-stick touch controls, or a controller
- Progress saves to your CrazyGames account

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Touch | Controller |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fly | WASD | Drag on the left half | Left stick |
| Aim | Mouse, or ← → / Q E to turn | Drag on the right half | Right stick |
| Chain guns | Left click or Space | Fire by themselves at anything ahead | Right trigger |
| Missiles | Right click or ↓ / K | MSL button | Left trigger |
| Flares | F | FLR button | Right bumper |
| Turbo | Shift | TRB button | Left bumper |
| Pause | P | Pause button | Start |
| Sound on/off | M | | Back |

Land on your base pad to repair and rearm. Keys can be changed in the game (C on the title screen or while paused).
